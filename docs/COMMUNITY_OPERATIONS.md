# Community operations

## Recommended process

Keep profile verification separate from publication. A member submits a profile, a reviewer confirms alumni status, then the member can submit stories or requests. New content and edits require another person's review. Reviewers must recuse themselves from their own, family-related or financially connected cases. Rejection reasons should explain a concrete correction and the appeal contact.

For blood requests, verify the requester, hospital, blood group, units, deadline and permission to publish contact details. Close a request when fulfilled. Do not publish a patient's private medical files. Volunteer donor status is self-reported availability, not a medical eligibility decision.

For fundraising, check purpose, beneficiary consent, target, currency, payment recipient and evidence. Display payment instructions only. Label progress as organizer-reported if added later. Ask the organizer to publish an outcome after closure. Never request card details, PINs, passwords or payment OTPs.

For achievement stories, confirm authorship and consent. Require attribution and permission for every photograph. Do not imply that the website owns submitted stories or images: the website footer credits the developer, while each contributor retains their material's rights.

For moderation, record the decision and reason. Resolve reports with an explanation, and close misleading posts while reviewing. Admin featuring should apply only to already approved records. A featured label is editorial selection, not financial endorsement.

## Example journey

A verified alumnus needs blood for a relative. They select Blood request, enter hospital, group, units, deadline and an authorized contact, and submit. A moderator checks those facts and approves. The request appears in the public feed. Anyone can share its link. The requester closes it after help is received.

A verified member celebrates graduation. They submit a story with a credited photograph. A reviewer approves it; an administrator may feature it. The member shares the public story link using Facebook or the device share menu.

A verified member starts a support campaign. For fundraising, they add the purpose, target, payment recipient and instructions, plus private verification notes for reviewers. After independent review the public page displays the instructions. Money never passes through the website.

## Decisions still owned by the association

Select the initial administrators, define acceptable alumni verification evidence, assign review responsibilities, set retention periods, and decide who handles appeals and deletion requests. These decisions do not require changing the code. Keep the existing draft constitution labeled as a draft until formally adopted.
